    <ol className="jakpot-landing__principles">
      <li><h3>Give space to breathe</h3><p>Use spacious layouts and generous spacing to help people feel comfortable, reduce visual overload, and make content easier to take in.</p></li>
      <li><h3>Keep it clear</h3><p>Use concise, direct copy. Every label, heading, and description should communicate what it needs to without unnecessary wording.</p></li>
      <li><h3>Build a familiar rhythm</h3><p>Repeat layout patterns and component behaviours so people can recognise structure, understand what to expect, and move through experiences comfortably.</p></li>
      <li><h3>Design for accessibility</h3><p>Make the system usable by people with different needs, abilities, and ways of interacting.</p></li>
      <li><h3>Build by combining</h3><p>Treat components as mix-and-match building blocks. Combine and arrange them to create different layouts and experiences without starting from scratch.</p></li>

      <p>JAKpot uses three typefaces, each with a distinct role.</p>
      <dl className="jakpot-landing__token-list">
        <div><dt>League Spartan</dt><dd>Display type for headings.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Lexend</dt><dd>Body type for paragraphs and general content.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Atkinson Hyperlegible Next</dt><dd>Supporting type for labels and compact text.</dd></div>
      </dl>
      <p className="jakpot-landing__supporting">Typography styles include hero and section headings, card headings, subheadings, large and medium descriptions, card descriptions, and image captions. Their sizes adapt at tablet and mobile breakpoints.</p>
    </div>
    <div className="jakpot-landing__subsection">
      <h3>Spacing and layout</h3>
      <p>The spacing scale follows an 8px grid, from 8px through 96px. Use the scale to create consistent gaps, padding, and section spacing. Layout tokens define the content width, while Section and Grid provide reusable width, column, and responsive behaviour.</p>
    </div>
    <div className="jakpot-landing__subsection">
      <h3>Borders, elevation, and motion</h3>
      <p>Use the radius scale for consistent corners, the three shadow levels to establish depth, and the fast and base transition tokens for interface feedback. Reduced-motion preferences are respected by the stylesheet.</p>

    <p>Provide the icon image source and appropriate alternative text. Available display sizes are 16, 18, 20, 24, and 32 pixels. Use icons to support a label or action, not as a substitute for clear wording.</p>
  </section>
